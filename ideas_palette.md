# Orange-Cherry-Cream-Peach Design System (SmartTrack v3)

## Theme Name
**Warm Terracotta / Sunset Dispatch**

## Very Brief Intro
A rich, warm palette inspired by artisanal ceramics and natural daylight. Deep cherry provides anchoring contrast, vibrant orange and peach supply warm operational emphasis, and warm cream creates a welcoming canvas.

## Design Movement
Warm Organic Modernism meets High-End Dispatch Editorial.

## Core Principles
1. **Warm Contrast**: Deep cherry (`#5D0703`) for high-contrast headers, primary text, and dark command surfaces.
2. **Vibrant Handoffs**: Energetic orange (`#FC703C`) and peach (`#FFA175`) for live route lines, vehicle markers, and interactive CTAs.
3. **Calm Atmosphere**: Cream (`#F4F3E6`) for page backgrounds, card surfaces, and subtle separation without clinical starkness.
4. **Human Touch**: Generous padding, soft shadows, rounded tactile controls, and integrated star-rating feedback.

## Color Palette
- **Cherry**: `#5D0703` (Primary dark, headers, deep panels)
- **Orange**: `#FC703C` (Primary accent, live buttons, vehicle markers)
- **Peach**: `#FFA175` (Secondary highlight, subtle gradients, warning badges)
- **Cream**: `#F4F3E6` (Warm canvas background, card base)
- **Muted Cream**: `#EAE8D4` (Borders, card dividers)
